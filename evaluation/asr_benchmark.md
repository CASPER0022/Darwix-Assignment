## Method

Every phrase is synthesised by TTS in a **native voice for its locale**, so the exact reference text is known and word error rate is a real measurement rather than an impression. Each phrase targets one specific difficulty — code-switching, a regional accent, a piece of market vocabulary — rather than being chosen to flatter the model.

**The honest caveat:** TTS audio is cleaner than a human on a mobile line in traffic. These numbers are a **best case**, and real-world WER will be higher. That is why the error analysis below matters more than the headline figure — the mistakes that survive on clean audio are the ones that will dominate on a real call.
