## Reading these numbers

- **ASR dominates.** It is a network call to a hosted Whisper endpoint; everything after it is local and costs single-digit milliseconds, except the LLM layer.
- **The rules layer is effectively free**, which is why every compliance-critical signal is detected there and never depends on a model call that might time out.
- **The LLM layer runs concurrently and debounced**, so its latency adds to its own nudges but never delays transcription or a compliance alert.
- **End to end is dominated by how long the speaker talks**, not by the pipeline: a segment is only transcribed once the utterance ends. That is a deliberate accuracy/latency trade and is discussed in `docs/limitations_and_production_plan.md`.
