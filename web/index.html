About this prototype

Built for a technical assessment. The knowledge base is grounded in the publicly published pages and policy PDFs of a real NBFC, and every answer cites the record it came from.

The credit policy, qualification matrix, objection handbook, application form and lead records are synthetic — authored for this exercise, labelled as such in every record and in every citation the agent speaks. The callers on all recorded test calls are synthetic too.

This system is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any company named in it. The agent discloses that it is an AI, and that the call is recorded, before it asks anything.
